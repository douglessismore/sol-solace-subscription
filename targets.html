Reality check on capacity

3 rooms × 8 working hours × 6 days = 144 hrs/wk theoretical max. Realistic sustainable utilization is more like 60–80 hrs/wk across all practitioners (the busy-Austin-studio benchmark). So anything under 35 hrs/wk is feasible without adding new practitioners; 35–55 is feasible with hiring or current ones increasing hours; above 55 likely needs structural growth.

How the math works

Every cell and result uses the same formula. If anything in these assumptions feels off, change it and watch the numbers shift.

Assumptions baked in

Rate: $200/hr for both Monica and practitioners

Practitioner split: 50/50 (studio keeps half of practitioner billings)

Working weeks/year: 50 (accounting for vacation/holidays)

Effective tax rate: 30% (rough — refine with Tammy)

Formula: Take-home = (Monica's revenue + 50% of practitioner revenue − studio overhead + products + membership) × 0.7

What this doesn't model

→ Year-1 ramp for products (they start at conservative for ~6–9 months before hitting realistic)

→ Seasonal variation (June–August dip; holiday surge)

→ Practitioner cost beyond the 50/50 split (admin, training, retention)

→ Marketing/ad spend ramp (already inside the product net numbers)

→ Capital outlay for product launch ($8–15K upfront, not in operating math)
