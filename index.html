What it would actually take.

Sol Solace at $158K/yr two years running, $190K trending for 2026. Here's what changes — and which combinations of moves would actually clear the bar to enable a move abroad.

The short version

Status quo doesn't fund the move — even with the 2026 trend. Takes-home lands around $25K, against a ~$150K target.

Three changes actually clear the bar: a membership that fills practitioner hours, a curanderismo-rooted product line, and reducing or eliminating the studio overhead.

The two paths that work best (E and F on the cashflow page) both involve downsizing the physical studio. The question isn't whether to change the model — it's how much.
