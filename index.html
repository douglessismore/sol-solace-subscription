What to build next at Sol Solace.

Three directions worth considering — sized realistically against today's numbers, and shaped so they survive a move abroad.

The short version

1. A membership for regulars. $185/mo autopay for a monthly practitioner session. Stabilizes near-term cashflow, fills empty rooms, keeps Monica's hour at $200.

2. A curanderismo-rooted product line. An annual cyclical-practice subscription (Solsticio) plus seasonal cultural boxes plus an apothecary. This is where the ceiling is — and it ships from anywhere, including Mexico.
