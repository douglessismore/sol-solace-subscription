Strategy Summary · May 2026

Sol Solace — where things stand.

Plateaued revenue, thin margins, and a stated goal (a move abroad) the current model can't fund. Three structural paths can change that. One chart, three paths, the open questions.

The picture

Sol Solace did $158K in gross sales in 2024, and $158K again in 2025 — flat year over year. 2026 is on pace for roughly $190K, driven by Monica taking more hours after Doug's layoff. Profit margin sits near 5%; rent is 25–29% of revenue. The current shape doesn't generate enough take-home to also save for the planned move to Mexico.

Where the math points

Both Reshape and Release clear the take-home target with room. Reshape produces slightly more total take-home and retains a physical studio + small team. Release simplifies operations, eliminates rent and management overhead, and is the cleanest fit for relocation logistics.

The deciding factors aren't financial — they're about studio identity, the team, Monica's apprenticeship direction, and what feels right to put a name on (the products question).

Open questions

  • Which path best matches Monica's current direction — the apprenticeship in Oaxaca, teaching/retreat ambitions, time/energy capacity?
  • What would be grieved letting go of: the physical studio? The practitioners? The "Sol Solace as a place" identity?
  • What's the move timeline window — 12 months, 18, 24? Earlier = faster decision on the studio.
  • Comfort level with a product line carrying Monica's name and lineage. Boundary on what's okay to commercialize.
  • Lease specifics — breakable, renewal date, downsize option in the current building?
  • Capital plan — where does $8–15K of product-launch inventory and setup come from?

Caveats on the numbers
