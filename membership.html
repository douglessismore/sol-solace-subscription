Path 01 · Service-side

The membership.

A predictable monthly retainer that fills empty practitioner hours — without touching the $200 rate or pulling Monica deeper into management.

Three versions worth considering

Ordered by how much each protects the premium positioning. The recommended pick (A) keeps Monica's $200 hour intact and routes demand to underused practitioners.

Recommended

A. Sol Solace Circle

$185/month · with any practitioner (not Monica)

One 60-min session per month with a practitioner, billed automatically. Small loyalty perks — 10% off retail, free monthly community sound bath, priority booking. Sessions roll over up to 2 months so missing one isn't a loss.

What it does

  • Fills underused practitioner hours
  • Predictable monthly revenue
  • Keeps the $200 rate fully intact
  • Survives a move — practitioners run it

Tradeoffs

  • Modest revenue per member
  • $15 discount absorbed by studio
  • Needs practitioner buy-in on rollover
Higher upside, more risk

B. Sanctuary Tier

$295–350/month · premium bundle with Monica

For top 15–25 clients. One 90-min/month with Monica, quarterly add-on (limpia, sound bath, tarot), 15% off everything else. Costs more than ad-hoc — value is in priority, relationship, curated extras.

What it does

  • Premium positioning, not a discount
  • Deep relationships with best clients
  • $3.5K–4K/yr per member

Tradeoffs

  • Pulls Monica deeper into hours
  • Doesn't help cost-strapped clients
  • Hard to fulfill once moved
Complement, not replacement

C. Community Circle

$60–85/month · group sessions only

Sound baths, breath work, herb circles, group limpia. 8–12 per session. A lower entry point that doesn't compete with the $200 hour, and builds the community/teaching muscle Monica wants for retreats.

What it does

  • Captures cost-strapped clients
  • Great group margins
  • Builds teaching/retreat muscle

Tradeoffs

  • 2–4 group offerings/month to run
  • Needs a practitioner who likes groups
  • Different audience — marketing lift
My pick

A + C, layered. Circle for regulars who want predictable 1:1 care. Community for people who want connection at a lower price. Skip Sanctuary for now — it pulls Monica deeper into hours her teaching/retreat ambition wants her out of.

Things to watch

→ Don't anchor down. Keep the discount modest (~7%) so the $200 rate stays the anchor, not the exception.

→ Sliding scale isn't being used. Same failure modes could hit a membership if positioning isn't intentional — say a quick survey of regulars first.

→ Move-abroad tether. A 12-month membership sold today implicitly promises 12 months of fulfillment. Decide upfront whether it survives a move (run by practitioners), winds down, or converts to digital.
