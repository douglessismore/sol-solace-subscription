Path 02 · Product line

The product line.

Authentic Mexican curanderismo, sold by an actual curandera. The market lane is wide open — and unlike a service business, this one gets bigger when you move abroad, not smaller.

Why this matters

Service revenue is capped by Monica's hours. Product revenue is capped by demand — and demand scales with ad spend and email list. 100 subscribers at $245/yr is $24K. 500 is $122K. 2,000 is $490K. The existing ~2,000-person email list is the launch audience. The realistic combined product-line scenario lands around $127K/yr net; stretch is ~$438K.

The architecture

One headline subscription, two seasonal cultural products, one always-on apothecary line. Each tier funnels into the next.

A Solsticio Year 1 customer renewing 3 years ≈ ~$605 lifetime value against ~$40 CAC. That LTV/CAC ratio is the reason this scales with FB/Meta ads.

Things to watch

  • Substance > aesthetic. Beautiful packaging is necessary but not sufficient. If 60% of budget is in written content (ceremony cards, instructions, recorded audio), you're doing it right. If reversed, it's an Instagram product.
  • Sourcing is the brand. Sustainable Oaxacan copal (overharvesting is real), ethically grown herbs, no appropriated white sage. COGS will be 30–35%, not 15%.
  • The refill model needs a yearly theme. Year 1 "Elements," Year 2 "Ancestors," Year 3 "Plants" — otherwise customers churn at year two.
  • $8–15K upfront capital. Inventory, packaging, photography, the keepsake calendar object itself. Real money before any revenue.
  • Monica's spiritual yes matters more than the business yes. Some practices/objects feel right to put on a shelf; some don't. She draws the line.
