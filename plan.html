How it sequences

The plan.

A 6-month path that runs the membership and the product line in parallel — but doesn't ask Monica to overhaul anything at once. The studio/lease question waits until month 6, after products have generated real data.

Immediate next milestone

Tuesday, June 2 — first cashflow review with the advisor. Walk through the cashflow scenarios. Goal: pressure-test the assumptions, get an outside read on which path makes the most sense given the timeline, and lock the recurring monthly meeting rhythm starting end of June.

  1. Weeks 1–2 · Decide & align

    Pick the membership structure and lock the numbers

    Confirm Circle at $185, Community at $75, rollover policy (2 months). Align practitioners on session economics in writing. Write the one-pager for clients.

  2. Weeks 2–4 · Soft-launch the membership

    Founding cohort — 20–25 members from the email list

    Square or Stripe recurring billing. Personal email/DM to most-engaged regulars. Frame as a founding cohort — locked pricing, gratitude for early support. Cap it. Goal: 20 paid signups in 2 weeks.

  3. Weeks 3–6 · Begin product prep (parallel)

    Solsticio research & sourcing

    Identify the keepsake calendar object (woodworker? potter? collaboration with a Mexican artisan?). Source copal and candle supplier. Decide Year 1 theme. Draft ceremony content (Monica). Photographer scouting. Budget inventory cost honestly.

  4. Weeks 6–10 · First product MVP

    Launch the apothecary singles first

    Start with the easiest SKUs — candles, copal, herb bundles, splash. Shopify store, basic product photography. Sell to the existing email list first. Use this to learn fulfillment before the big Solsticio launch. Goal: 200 orders in 6 weeks, validate operations.

  5. Weeks 8–14 · Solsticio production

    Manufacture & photograph the inaugural Year 1 boxes

    Order keepsake calendars in batch (long lead time). Produce ceremony cards, finalize Year 1 contents. Big photoshoot. Build the landing page using the mockup as reference. Pre-orders open week 12 for fall delivery.

  6. Week 12 · Two-month review

    Look at actual numbers with the advisor

    Two months of membership data + apothecary data. Compare to projections. Decide whether membership keeps, adjusts, or winds down. Decide whether to add Community tier publicly. Set production scale for Solsticio based on apothecary response.

  7. Late Sep · Día de los Muertos box drop

    First seasonal cultural product

    Limited run, sells through the Oct 24–Nov 2 window. Test what cultural-specific product looks like vs the mass-market Solsticio.

  8. Mid Nov · Posadas Box + Solsticio launch

    Holiday season — full lineup live

    Solsticio Year 1 ships for Winter Solstice; Posadas Box ships for Dec 16. FB ads scaling against learnings from the apothecary test. Full architecture live for end-of-year gift season.

Open questions

Things this doc can't answer without input from Monica and the practitioners — worth talking through before anything ships.

  • Practitioner buy-in on Circle. Are they up for the rollover/scheduling implications? Autopay roster, or stay walk-in?
  • Per-practitioner capacity. How many more hours/wk can each one realistically take? Sets the ceiling on Circle membership.
  • Move-abroad timing. Best guess on the window? Changes how aggressively to sell long-term commitments and how to sequence Mexico-side fulfillment.
  • Monica's read on commercializing curanderismo. Which practices/objects feel right to put on a shelf? Which absolutely don't?
  • Solsticio Year 1 theme. Elements? Ancestors? Plants? What feels grounded and lands with launch energy?
  • The keepsake calendar object. Wooden, ceramic, fabric, leather? Made by whom? Centerpiece of the product line's aesthetic identity.
  • Tivra's interest. Tarot membership? Featured in apothecary as a reading add-on at checkout?
  • Sliding-scale data. Why aren't people using the current sliding-scale offering? A quick survey of regulars could de-risk the whole launch.
  • Email list health. Of the ~2,000 subscribers, how many opened the last 3 emails? That's the realistic addressable audience.
  • Capital plan. The product line needs $8–15K upfront. Savings, business cash flow, small loan, or staged out of apothecary revenue once running?
